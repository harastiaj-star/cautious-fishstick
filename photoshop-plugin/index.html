dither

Dots, pixels or engraved lines in 1 to 3 inks, on a new layer.

Type a hex color like #1f3b4d, or "transparent" for the background.

Open a document and press Preview.

pixel art

Cuts out the subject and redraws it as chunky pixels in one ink, on a new layer.

Type "transparent" for paper to get the pixels alone.

Open a document and press Preview.

trailhead filters

Runs a Trailhead photo tool on your document, on a new layer.

textures

Adds the texture on a clear layer. Try Multiply.

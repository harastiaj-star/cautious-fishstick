TRAILHEAD

tools for making things by hand
Ready when you are. Messages and errors show up here.

open a download

Pick anything you saved from the Trailhead site. Zips are fine, there's no need to unzip them first.

pocket scanner

Use your phone as a scanner. Press Pair a phone, scan the code with your phone's camera, and any photo you send from that page lands here.

Not paired.

Photos are locked on your phone and only this panel has the key. They pass through ntfy.sh, which deletes them within three hours.

palettes

Tap a color to make it your foreground color.

colors from this document

Finds the main colors in the open document, like Image colors on the site.

trailhead filters

Runs a Trailhead photo tool on everything visible in your document and puts the result on a new layer. Your original layers stay as they are.

textures

Adds a new layer the size of your document with the texture in ink on a clear background. Try Multiply or lower the opacity.
